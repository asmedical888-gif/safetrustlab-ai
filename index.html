FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Safe Trust AI make absolute authenticity decisions?+
No. Safe Trust AI produces probabilistic risk indicators, evidence-linked signals, and reviewable reports designed to support human decision-making.
Can Safe Trust AI run privately?+
Private and hybrid deployment are part of the roadmap for customers with sensitive data and strict infrastructure requirements.
Does Safe Trust AI store customer videos?+
Data retention should be configured according to customer requirements and deployment model. The platform is designed to support configurable retention policies.
Is Safe Trust AI an NVIDIA partner?+
No. NVIDIA technology is referenced as part of our planned acceleration and deployment roadmap. We plan to evaluate NVIDIA GPUs, DGX Spark, RTX systems, CUDA, TensorRT-LLM, NVIDIA NIM, and NVIDIA AI Enterprise.
What is the difference between cloud, hybrid, and private deployment?+
Cloud is fastest for pilots, hybrid keeps sensitive workflows customer-controlled while using approved cloud services, and private deployment is designed for local inference and customer-controlled infrastructure.
Who is this built for?+
Enterprise HR, remote onboarding, legal/compliance review, online education, professional services, government/high-security organizations, and family offices handling sensitive data.

Privacy Policy

Safe Trust AI is operated through SAFE FRONT, Inc. in the United States and SAFE TRUST LAB AI PTE. LTD. in Singapore. This policy explains, in plain terms, how we approach data in our product and on this website.

Last updated: 2026. This summary is provided for transparency and is not a statement of formal legal certification.

PDPA-aware data handling

We design our workflows for PDPA-aware data handling. References to the PDPA describe our design intent and operational practices; they are not a claim of certification or guaranteed regulatory compliance. Enterprise deployments are configured to support PDPA-aware handling appropriate to the customer's requirements.

Configurable retention

Data retention is configurable according to customer requirements and deployment model (cloud, hybrid, or private). Retention periods, storage location, and deletion schedules are set per engagement rather than assumed by default.

Uploaded media & evidence handling

Uploaded interview media, keyframes, audio metadata, source records, and generated reports are handled according to the agreed retention configuration. For sensitive workflows, private and hybrid deployment options are designed to keep media and evidence under customer-controlled infrastructure.

Demo outputs are illustrative

The interactive demo on this website uses illustrative sample values to show the product workflow. Demo outputs are probabilistic risk indicators and do not represent real analysis of any uploaded file.

Human review required

Safe Trust AI produces probabilistic risk indicators and evidence-linked signals designed to support human decision-making. Outputs are not absolute determinations of authenticity and are intended to be reviewed by a human before any decision is made.
